About This Practice

The "What Went Well" exercise is based on research in Positive Psychology by Martin Seligman and Angela Duckworth. By identifying three good things each day and reflecting on your role in making them happen, you:

  • Cultivate awareness of positive experiences
  • Recognize your personal agency and autonomy
  • Build a growth mindset by connecting your actions to positive outcomes
  • Develop resilience and optimism
