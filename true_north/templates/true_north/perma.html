PERMA

PERMA maps the five building blocks of a flourishing life, helping people notice, strengthen, and balance what makes their well-being last.

P

Positive Emotion

Regularly feeling joy, gratitude, hope, and contentment broadens your outlook and builds a reserve of resilience you can draw on when life gets hard.

Ways to practice

  • Savoring a good meal
  • Gratitude journaling
  • Music you love
  • Time outdoors
  • Celebrating small wins
E

Engagement

Losing yourself in an activity that stretches your skills produces “flow” — a deep absorption that grows your strengths and makes time seem to disappear.

Ways to practice

  • Playing an instrument
  • Solving a hard problem
  • Gardening
  • Painting or making
  • Sport that absorbs you
R

Relationships

Warm, supportive connections give you a sense of belonging and someone to lean on, and they turn out to be one of the strongest predictors of well-being.

Ways to practice

  • Calling a friend
  • Sharing a meal
  • Helping a neighbor
  • Honest conversation
  • Joining a group or team
M

Meaning

Belonging to and serving something larger than yourself gives life direction and purpose, so daily effort feels worthwhile even when it is difficult.

Ways to practice

  • Volunteering
  • Mentoring someone
  • Faith or reflection
  • Creative work
  • Contributing to a cause
A

Accomplishment

Setting goals and making real progress toward them builds competence and pride, giving you the confidence that you can shape outcomes through your own effort.

Ways to practice

  • Finishing a project
  • Learning a new skill
  • Hitting a fitness goal
  • Mastering a craft
  • Meeting a deadline
